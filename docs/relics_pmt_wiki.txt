^ Parameter              ^ Condition         ^
| Dark Count Rate        | < 1200 Hz          |
| SPE Gain (HV @ 800 V)  | > 2.0 × 10⁶ e⁻    |
| Energy Resolution      | < 0.5              |
| After-Pulse Probability| Exclude any PMT with a record > 5%; retain if no APP data |
